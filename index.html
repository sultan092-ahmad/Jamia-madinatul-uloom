Deen and duniya, learned side by side.

Jamia Madinatul Uloom is a centre of Islamic learning in Phakuli Shareef , established by Hazrat Namazi Shah R.A . Students study the Quran , Arabic and the Islamic sciences alongside modern subjects , in a setting built around adab , discipline and care.

Our aim is to nurture children with the teachings of the Quran and the values of Islam , helping them grow with faith , knowledge , good character and a sense of responsibility towards their families , community & while preparing them for both this world and the Aakhirah.

Adab

Respect for teachers , parents and one another comes before marks and grades.

Ilm

Careful , steady study of the Quran and the deen , one lesson at a time.

Khidmat

Serving family and community is part of every student's training.

Programs for every stage

Choose a program to see what students study and who it suits.

Hifz-e-Quran

Students memorise the Quran under experienced teachers, with equal importance given to correct recitation, regular revision and understanding the honour of the Quran.

  • Daily new lesson (Sabaq)
  • Recent revision (Sabqi)
  • Old revision (Manzil)
  • Correct pronunciation with Tajweed
  • Basic duas and daily prayers

Suited for :- Children who can already read the Quran and want to begin memorisation.

Alimiyyah

The classical course of Islamic sciences, taught step by step over several years for students who wish to develop deeper knowledge of Deen.

  • Arabic language and grammar
  • Tafsir
  • Hadith
  • Fiqh
  • Aqidah and Islamic history

Suited for :- Students who have completed the primary stage and want deeper Islamic education.

Qari

A Quran-focused course designed to help students develop correct Quran recitation, Tajweed and a beautiful style of reading, with proper pronunciation and understanding of essential recitation rules.

  • Quran recitation with correct pronunciation
  • Tajweed rules and their practical application
  • Makharij and correct articulation of Arabic letters
  • Memorisation and revision of selected Surahs
  • Improvement of fluency and recitation skills

Suited for :- Students who wish to improve their Quran recitation and learn to recite the Quran correctly according to the principles of Tajweed.

Modern Subjects

Modern subjects are taught alongside Islamic education so that students can continue their academic journey with confidence.

  • English reading and writing
  • Mathematics
  • General Science
  • Hindi
  • Basic Computer Skills & Computer Lab

Suited for :- Students who want to continue modern education alongside their Deeni studies.

A day at the Jamia

The day is built around the five prayers , so timings move a little through the year.

  1. After Fajr

    Recitation and revision

    The day begins with Quran recitation and a review of yesterday's lessons, while the mind is fresh.

  2. Morning

    Main classes

    Quran, Arabic and deeniyat, taught in small groups by level.

  3. After Zuhr

    Meal and rest

    Lunch together, then a short rest before the afternoon.

  4. Afternoon

    Modern subjects

    English, mathematics, Hindi and computer basics.

  5. After Asr

    Play and fresh air

    Sports and outdoor time, because a healthy body helps a steady mind.

  6. After Maghrib

    Guided self-study

    Students prepare tomorrow's lessons with a teacher nearby to help.

  7. After Isha

    Dinner, dua and sleep

    The day closes with the evening duas, and early rest for an early start.

How to join

Four simple steps , and we are happy to talk through any of them.

  1. Send an enquiry

    Tell us which program interests you , using the form or by phone.

  2. Visit the Jamia

    Meet the teachers , see the classrooms and ask your questions.

  3. Short assessment

    A friendly reading check helps us place the student in the right class.

  4. Submit documents and begin

    Bring the student's identity papers and photographs and start classes.
